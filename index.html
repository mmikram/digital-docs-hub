Digital Docs Hub - Account Deletion

If you registered an account on Digital Docs Hub using Google Sign-In and wish to permanently delete your account and data, you can do so in-app or via this form.

Warning: Deleting your account will permanently purge your Cloud Firestore profile, subscription records, and Google authentication token linkage.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion

  1. Open the Digital Docs Hub app on your Android device.
  2. Open My Account Profile from the main screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm the prompt.

Option 2: Web Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app, submit your registered Gmail address below or email support@theneeds.com.
